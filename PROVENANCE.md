# VAYU provenance and claims boundary

> Naming note: the product is **VAYU** (the orchestrator). Its engine package is named
> `monsoon_grid` internally (the original working title). Separately, VAYU can optionally
> read hash-verified weather context from an **external climate-context adapter** (a distinct
> climate-twin project); that adapter is referred to below as the "weather-context adapter"
> to avoid confusion with the product name. Script and path identifiers such as
> `discover_vayu_assets.ps1` retain their original names.

VAYU is independently authored under this directory. The demo portfolio, profiles, events, forecasts, prices, and carbon factor are synthetic or scenario assumptions; they are not plant telemetry and do not establish forecast accuracy, savings, reliability, market value, regulatory compliance, or control authority.

The weather-context adapter is disabled by default and accepts only a strict, hash-verified weather-context record. It never imports external climate-twin modules, reads checkpoints, writes the external tree, or supplies authoritative power, demand, BESS, line, price, action, or safety fields. The optional bench is MOCK-ONLY and makes no HIL/CHIL/PHIL or live-control claim.

`runtime/provenance/protected_manifest_before.json` and `runtime/provenance/protected_manifest_after.json` are generated by `scripts/discover_vayu_assets.ps1`. The inventory includes the protected external climate-twin trees, archives, reports, archive directory, and supplied `.venv`; product-owned runtime files are excluded. A comparison failure is a release failure. `runtime/verification-note.md` is the judge-facing record of the actual validation run.

Decision certificates are Ed25519 signed only when `cryptography` is installed and an externally supplied keyring, validity time, and pinned fingerprint are present. The local wrapper points explicitly at the separate `trust/` demo fixture for this isolated synthetic demonstration; production use must replace it with an operator-controlled path. Missing trust, validity, evidence, content-store, SQLite, or signing dependencies fail closed for certificate verification.
